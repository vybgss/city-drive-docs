Privacy Policy

City Drive is built to keep your data with you. We don't collect it, and we don't track you.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Summary

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • We do not collect, store or share any personal information.
  • The app contains no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking.
  • Your lesson records stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

1. Information we collect

None. City Drive does not ask for, collect or transmit any personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or device identifiers. We have no servers that receive data from the app.

2. Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your device using Apple's standard app storage:

  • your lesson records: best stars, best times and fastest laps;
  • your settings: language, sound, graphics and gameplay options;
  • which lesson packs you have unlocked, so they open instantly on launch.

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is removed from the device when you delete the app.

3. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your lesson records are synced between your devices through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, in your own private iCloud account. Settings are not synced. We cannot see or access this data. iCloud is provided by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, and you can turn off iCloud for City Drive in your device's iCloud settings at any time.

4. In-app purchases

Lesson packs are optional one-time purchases processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details or Apple Account information. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, which packs you own.

5. Permissions

City Drive does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or other personal data, and it does not ask for permission to track you.

6. Diagnostics

The app does not include any third-party analytics or crash-reporting services. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you.

7. Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party services, shows no advertising and does not share data with third parties.

8. Children's privacy

City Drive is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
