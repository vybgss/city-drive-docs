Why City Drive

01

Step by step

Every lesson is a checklist of real actions: seatbelt, engine, gear, handbrake. Hints tell you when to signal, brake and turn.

02

Real road rules

Traffic lights, STOP lines, priority roads, give-way signs, speed limits and traffic around you. Every slip is counted.

03

Feels like a real car

Automatic P-R-N-D gearbox, analog pedals, indicators that cancel after the turn and a wheel that recenters by itself.

04

Mirrors and shoulder checks

Live rear-view and side mirrors. Swipe to turn your head or look over your shoulder, double-tap to hold the view.

05

Park like a pro

Perpendicular, parallel and angle parking, pulling out and a tight garage, guided by mirror reference points.

06

Ice and skids

On ice, braking takes three times longer and the car slides easily. Learn to be smooth, then take it to the race track.

07

Stars and records

Up to three stars per lesson, your best time and fastest lap. Records sync across your devices with iCloud.

08

Private and offline

No account, no ads, no tracking. City Drive plays fully offline, in seven languages.
