Lessons

How do I start a lesson?

Swipe through the lesson cards on the main screen and tap Start. Each card shows a blueprint of the maneuver, your best stars and your best time.

How do I complete a lesson?

The panel on the left shows your current task. Do the steps in order, just like in a real car: fasten the seatbelt, start the engine, select D, release the handbrake, drive, and finish as instructed, for example by stopping, selecting P and setting the handbrake.

Hints along the way tell you when to signal, brake or turn. New to the game? Begin with Controls, the first lesson, on an empty practice ground.

Why did my run end?

A run ends if you crash into a car, a wall or another obstacle, leave the road, or run out of time in a timed lesson. Leaving the road is judged by every corner of the car, so even letting the body hang over a kerb counts.

After each run the camera lifts above your car, so you can see exactly what you touched or how straight you parked. Tap Retry to try again right away.

What counts as a mistake?

Things a driving examiner would note, for example: driving without the seatbelt, turning or changing lanes without the indicator, running a red light or a STOP sign, speeding, crossing a solid line, driving in the oncoming lane, failing to give way, turning in front of oncoming traffic, following too closely, changing lanes without checking your blind spot, and reacting slowly or braking too softly in an emergency stop.

Mistakes show as warnings while you drive and are listed on the result panel. To see them only at the end, turn on Hide mistakes while driving in Settings.

Tap a mistake on the result panel to see where it happened on the map and a short explanation of the rule. Tap it again to jump to the next time you made the same mistake.

How are stars awarded?

A run with no mistakes earns ★★★, one or two mistakes earn two stars, and three or more earn one star. A crash earns no stars. Your best result for each lesson is kept, so you can replay any lesson to improve it.

On the race tracks traffic rules are not checked. There your record is the fastest lap.

Driving

How do I steer?

Drag the knob of the steering slider in the bottom-left corner left or right. Near the center, small finger movements turn the wheels only a little, so it is easy to drive straight; toward the ends the steering gets quicker, and the end of the slider is full lock. When you let go, it slides back to center on its own, faster the faster you drive, just like a real car straightening out. When the car is standing still, it stays where you left it.

The steering wheel in the cabin, in front of the instruments, turns with you: one and a half turns from lock to lock. You can also steer by tilting your device, see Can I steer by tilting my device? below.

How do the pedals work?

Gas and Brake in the bottom-right corner are analog. The higher you touch the pedal, the deeper it is pressed: the top third is full travel, the bottom edge is a light touch. Slide your finger up or down to adjust without letting go. The colored fill shows how far the pedal is pressed.

What do the switches and the gear selector do?

The switches at the top are Engine, Handbrake, Seatbelt and the Left and Right indicators. An indicator turns itself off when the wheel returns to center after a turn.

The car has an automatic gearbox. Use the P-R-N-D selector in the top-right corner: D to drive forward, R to reverse. Change gear only when the car has stopped.

How do I look around or over my shoulder?

Swipe left or right on the road view to turn your head. Swiping right goes all the way over your shoulder to the rear window, which helps when reversing. Swipe up to look up, for example at a high traffic light. Let go and your view returns forward.

To keep looking aside while you drive, double-tap the view with a second finger while you look. Double-tap again to unlock. The rear-view and side mirrors always show live traffic behind you.

Lesson packs and purchases

Which lessons are free?

The first 19 lessons are free. The other 11 come in four optional packs:

  • Parking Pack: Perpendicular, Parallel and Angle Parking, Pulling Out, Garage
  • Zigzag Pack: Speed Zigzag, Slippery Road
  • Town Pack: Small Town, Small Town in Winter
  • Racing Pack: Race Track, Winter Race Track
How do I buy a pack?

Tap Unlock on any locked lesson card. The pack screen shows the price in your currency and the lessons it opens. Each pack is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it unlocks on all your devices with the same Apple Account.

If the purchase needs approval through Ask to Buy, the lessons open as soon as it's approved.

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Tap Unlock on a locked lesson card and then Restore purchase on the pack screen. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy the pack.

The purchase did not go through. What can I do?

Check your internet connection and try again. If you see a message that purchases are not allowed, check that in-app purchases are allowed in Screen Time on your device.

For refunds and billing questions, use reportaproblem.apple.com, since Apple handles all payments. For anything else, write to us.

Settings and progress

Is my progress saved and synced?

Yes. Your best stars, best times and fastest laps are saved automatically and sync between your devices through iCloud when you're signed in with the same Apple Account and iCloud is on. Settings are kept separately on each device.

How do I change the language?

Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner of the main screen and choose Language. City Drive is available in English, German, French, Ukrainian, Spanish, Japanese and Portuguese. By default it uses your device's language if supported, and English otherwise.

The game is slow or my device gets warm. What can I change?

Open Settings and look under Graphics:

  • Frame rate: 30 Hz uses less battery and stays cooler than 60 Hz.
  • Anti-aliasing: Medium or None is lighter than Strong.
  • Shadows: keep them off on older devices.
  • Depth fog changes the look of the scene. Field of view is Standard 72° by default; Wide 78° also shows the right mirror.

New graphics settings apply from the next lesson you start.

Can I turn off the sound?

Yes. Open Settings › Sound and switch off Sound effects. The Voice instructor, which reads tasks, hints and mistakes aloud, has its own switch there. Both follow your device's silent switch.

Can I drive on the left, like in the UK, Japan or Australia?

Yes. Open Settings › Gameplay and set Traffic drives on the to Left. The whole world is mirrored: you sit on the right, traffic keeps left, and lesson names, tasks and the maps on the lesson cards follow. It is chosen automatically if your device's region drives on the left, and applies from the next lesson you start. The first lesson, Controls, also asks at the end which side you want to drive on.

Can the speedometer show mph?

Yes. Open Settings › Gameplay and choose mph under Speed units. The speedometer, the speed-limit signs and the speeding warnings switch to miles per hour, with limits rounded to 5 mph like real signs. The trip odometer and the distance on the result screen show miles too; distances in task texts stay in metres and kilometres.

Can I steer by tilting my device?

Yes. Open Settings › Gameplay and set Steering to Tilt, then turn your device like a steering wheel: about 40° is full lock. The slider is hidden in this mode, and the wheel in the cabin still turns with you. Steering sensitivity changes how strongly the car reacts to small movements. The first lesson, Controls, also lets you try Slider and Tilt while driving and keep the one you like.

How do I pause, and what happens if I get a call?

Tap the pause button next to the timer. The lesson also pauses on its own when you get a call, open Control Center or switch apps. From the pause panel you can continue, retry, open Settings or go back to the menu.

Can I watch my run again or share it?

Yes. On the result panel, tap the round play button to watch a replay from above, at ½×, 1× or 2× speed, and scrub to any moment. Tap the share button in the replay to record it as a video; Apple's preview then lets you save or send it.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings › Progress and tap Reset. This deletes your stars, best times and fastest laps on this device and in iCloud. Purchased lesson packs stay unlocked. The same page shows whether iCloud sync is on.

General

Does City Drive need an internet connection?

No. City Drive works fully offline. A connection is only needed to buy or restore lesson packs and to sync your records through iCloud.

What are the requirements?

City Drive runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later. It plays full screen in landscape.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with your device model, iOS version, the app version shown at the bottom of Settings (for example v2.2 (98)), the lesson name and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
